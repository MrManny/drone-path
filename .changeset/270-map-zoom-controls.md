The flight map zooms in and out with a small `−`/`+` pill below the map (split view) and the `+` / `-` keys: every step pins the level as the fixed "Zoom" setting, so the export renders exactly the zoom the preview shows. Zooming clamps at the map's usable levels with the matching button disabling at either end, and opening a recording resets Zoom to auto-fit.
